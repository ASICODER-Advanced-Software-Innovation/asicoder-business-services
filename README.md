## 4. Personal Business Contact Data & Responsible Processing

**Business information is not automatically non-personal information.**

A company name or generic corporate email address may have a different privacy status from an identifiable employee's direct work email, phone number, or professional profile.

ASICODER's public compliance information addresses:

- Data sourcing and provenance
- Business information verification
- Corporate versus personal contact information
- Legal basis and permissions
- Country-specific restrictions
- Data subject rights
- Removal and suppression procedures

## Development & Business Research Workflow

### Step 1 — Define the Requirement

Explain your business problem, project objectives, intended users or research question.

### Step 2 — Define Scope

Identify the required software features, geographic markets, technologies, budget or expected deliverables.

### Step 3 — Review Feasibility

ASICODER reviews the requested work, available sources, technical considerations and applicable restrictions.

### Step 4 — Agree the Work

Project-specific scope, pricing, responsibilities and delivery arrangements are discussed before approved work proceeds.

### Step 5 — Delivery or Reporting

Deliverables depend on the agreed project and may include software development, documentation, design work, integration work or research findings.
