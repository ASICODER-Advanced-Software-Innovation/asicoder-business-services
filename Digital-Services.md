## How a Service Project Works

1. Describe the project requirements.
2. Identify the preferred technologies, features and expected outcomes.
3. Share an approximate budget and timeline where possible.
4. Allow the requirements to be reviewed.
5. Discuss a suitable scope, proposal and delivery approach.
6. Approve applicable commercial terms before development begins.
7. Review progress and deliverables according to the agreed project.

A submitted request is an inquiry, not an automatically accepted contract.
