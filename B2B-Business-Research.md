## What Is B2B Business Research?

B2B business research is the process of gathering, assessing and organizing relevant business information to understand companies, industries, geographic markets and commercial opportunities.

ASICODER supports research inquiries focused on company discovery, geographic markets, business information review, industry context and structured research reporting.

The purpose is to provide useful business context rather than assume every requested contact or dataset is available.

---

## B2B Company Discovery

Company discovery focuses on identifying businesses that match defined research criteria.

Criteria may include:

- Country
- State or province
- City
- Industry
- Business category
- Commercial activity
- Public company website
- Relevant business characteristics

The quality of the results depends on the available sources, scope and verification methods.

---

## Country-Based Business Research

Country research provides a broad understanding of commercial activity within a national market.

Possible objectives include:

- Industry discovery
- Business landscape analysis
- Supplier research
- Market comparison
- Company identification
- Business expansion research

A country-level overview can help determine which regions require more detailed investigation.

---

## State and Province Research

Regional research helps narrow a business inquiry to selected administrative areas.

Examples include:

- Business activity in a state
- Regional supplier discovery
- Companies within a province
- Industry concentrations
- Geographic market comparisons

---

## City-Level Company Research

City-based research is useful when the focus is a local or metropolitan business environment.

Examples include:

- Local company discovery
- City-specific industry research
- Competitor research
- Local supplier identification
- Regional business mapping

Available data varies by country, city, industry and public source coverage.

---

## Industry and Competitor Research

Business research can examine companies participating in a particular market.

Research may review:

- Relevant company categories
- Industry activity
- Public business websites
- Geographic presence
- Business models
- Market positioning
- Available company information

Findings should distinguish verified details from assumptions.

## Business Information Verification

Verification may involve reviewing available source material, including public company websites and suitable business information sources.

A company record may be checked for:

- Company name
- Website
- Industry information
- Business location
- Public corporate contact channels
- Source and verification date

**Verification does not automatically establish permission to resell personal contact information.**

---

## Corporate Data vs Personal Professional Data

Corporate information and identifiable personal information must be treated differently.

### Examples of Corporate Information

- Company name
- Company website
- Industry classification
- Business location
- General corporate contact channels

### Examples of Potentially Personal Data

- Named employee details
- Direct professional email addresses
- Direct phone numbers
- Personal professional profiles
- Sole-trader contact details

Access, processing, disclosure and commercial use depend on applicable laws, sourcing and permissions.
